This policy explains how Novaamind AI Solutions ("Novaamind", "we", "us") handles personal data collected through this website. We process personal data in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

## What we collect

When you fill in a form on this site we collect what you enter: your name, work email, company, and role. If you choose to share them, we also collect your phone number, company size, and a message. We also record which page the form was on.

We do not use advertising trackers. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability.

## Why we use it

- To respond to your discovery session request or enquiry.
- To send you a resource you asked for, or notify you when it is published.
- To send insights by email if you subscribed. You can unsubscribe at any time.

We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for their own marketing.

## Where it is stored

Form submissions are stored with service providers that host our website, database, and email (for example Vercel, Supabase, and Resend). They process data only on our instructions.

## How long we keep it

We keep enquiry data for as long as needed to respond and maintain a business relationship, and no longer than three years after our last contact unless the law requires otherwise.

## Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent for emails, by writing to **raghav@novaamind.com**. We will respond within 30 days.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
