A Forward Deployed Engineer, or FDE, is a Novaamind engineer who joins your organisation full-time and works inside your team. They are on site, in your reviews, and in your systems. Their job is to find the operational problems that matter most and build the software that fixes them.

A typical consultant interviews your people, writes a recommendation, and leaves the building to someone else. An FDE stays and builds. They are measured on whether the system works and whether people use it.

We work this way because the hardest part of AI in a real business is understanding the work. Which numbers does the regional manager check first? Why does the month-end report take nine days? What does the store team do when the system shows the wrong figure? These answers come from being on the ground every day.

An FDE brings data, integration, AI, and application development skills. The wider Novaamind team supports them with architecture, code review, and specialist work.
