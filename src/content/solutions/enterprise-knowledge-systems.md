In most organisations, the answer to a routine question already exists somewhere. It might be in an SOP from 2022, a policy PDF on a shared drive, a WhatsApp thread, or the memory of the person who has been there longest. Finding it takes longer than it should, so people ask a colleague, guess, or improvise.

The cost is easy to miss and still real. Customers get a different experience in different locations. Onboarding is slow. The same mistakes repeat. Senior people spend hours answering questions they have answered many times before.

General-purpose AI chat tools cannot fix this on their own. They do not know your policies, they cannot tell who is allowed to see what, and they answer confidently even when they are wrong. An enterprise knowledge system is built on your content, follows your access rules, and shows where each answer came from.
