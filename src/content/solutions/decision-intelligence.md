Most organisations have plenty of data. What they lack is a way to bring it into decisions while those decisions still matter. Reports arrive after the moment has passed. Finance and operations bring numbers that do not match. A monthly review describes what happened, and people leave the room without agreeing on what to do.

Business intelligence tools made data visible. A dashboard with forty charts still leaves the hardest work to whoever has time to dig: noticing what matters, understanding why it happened, and choosing a response.

Decision intelligence starts from the decisions a business makes again and again. It designs the information each decision needs and uses AI to do the first round of noticing and explaining. People still make the call. They make it sooner, with better evidence, and with everyone looking at the same numbers.
